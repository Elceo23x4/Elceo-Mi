# Crowd Pain / Positioning Stress Map

Status: **IFP-6 active; architectural acceptance pending.** Policy: `positioning-stress-v1`.

## Two independent layers

The market-stress layer describes observed expectation failure, path stress, persisted volatility expansion, related-market conflict, IFP-4 cleanliness, IFP-5 narrative dislocation, and qualified IFP-2 analog context. It does not describe trader holdings. The direct-positioning layer is populated only by qualified, named positioning cohorts. Missing direct evidence remains unavailable and never becomes balanced or directional.

## Component policy

The visible weights are engineering constants: expectation/reality 15, path/whipsaw 20, volatility 15, related markets 10, cleanliness 15, narrative 15, and analog context 10. Unavailable evidence has zero effective weight and reduces coverage; it is not a zero observation and remaining weights are not renormalized. A state requires usable IFP-1 evidence, eligible IFP-4 and IFP-5 lineage, qualified provenance, and at least 0.75 coverage. Boundaries (25/50/75) are not empirical or predictive calibration claims.

## COT cohorts and availability

Legacy `non_commercial` is explicitly coarse. Disaggregated Producer/Merchant, Swap Dealer, Managed Money, and Other Reportables remain separate. TFF Dealer, Asset Manager, Leveraged Funds, and Other Reportables remain separate. No universal crowd total is constructed. A Tuesday observation is usable only at its published/available timestamp; report date is not knowledge time. The provider remains inactive.

## Orientation and historical baseline

`positioning-orientation-v1` explicitly maps supported CFTC contracts. EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, NZD/USD, XAU/USD, and BTC/USD are same-oriented; USD/JPY, USD/CHF, and USD/CAD are inverse-oriented. Unsupported mappings are structurally unavailable; symbol text is never interpreted.

Crowding uses canonical net divided by open interest against at most 156 prior eligible observations of the identical provider market, report kind, and cohort. At least 52 prior observations are required. The empirical percentile excludes the current observation and every future/late-available observation. Percentiles below 10 and at least 90 are short/long crowded; 10–20 and 80–90 are skewed; 20–80 is balanced. These are policy thresholds, not predictive calibration.

## Pain, provenance, and limitations

Crowding alone is latent. Side-specific pressure additionally requires actual adverse canonical-asset movement from IFP-1, including its persisted volatility adjustment. Fixture and unverified evidence are provenance limited. Verified/replay evidence needs a verification reference, trust basis, and verification time within the source-owned cutoff; advancing the evaluation cutoff cannot promote a failed source. Duplicate semantic observations are deduplicated and opposing qualified cohorts remain mixed rather than averaged.

IFP-2 outcomes supply context only and never current positions or a pressured side. IFP-4 ambiguity and hard conflicts and IFP-5 insufficiency, provenance, recovery, and reversal limitations remain visible. Outputs are descriptive market cognition, contain no execution instruction, and make no profitability or subsequent-direction promise.
