# IFP-3 Confidence-Zero Impact Diagnosis

The IFP charter records that 25 of 33 deterministic golden scenarios currently produce confidence zero. IFP-3 does not alter confidence formulas, confidence bands, confidence floors, contradiction penalties, golden inputs, or golden expected outputs.

Zero confidence currently appears when existing components exhaust or suppress confidence through missing evidence, severe contradiction, freshness penalties, readiness penalties, or other already-existing confidence anatomy components. These fixture outcomes are deterministic regression evidence only; they are not calibration acceptance or production correctness.

The protocol treats zero confidence as contextual diagnosis. Zero confidence alone cannot produce `invalidate_thesis` because invalidation requires direct persisted canonical invalidation evidence. Zero confidence alone cannot produce `escalate_review` because escalation requires sufficiently sourced direct current critical or compound contradiction evidence.

Zero-confidence cases therefore resolve according to evidence-supported state selection: immature evidence waits, material contradiction reviews, confirmed invalidation invalidates, and final resolved evidence archives. Remaining empirical limitations include the lack of production calibration proof and the need to inspect live provenance once external readiness blockers close.
