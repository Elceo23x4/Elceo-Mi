# Production provider certification: PROV-P1B

Reviewed 2026-09-07. Source: operator entitlement review and Tiingo account classification.

- Free credentials are evaluation credentials only. `evaluation_no_store` is not production entitlement.
- Tiingo market price history for EUR/USD is the only executable P1B profile. NewsAPI Developer is not staging-eligible; every other provider remains excluded.
- Normal runtime remains fixture-based and live-disabled. Evaluation is a short-lived, operator-only certification process.
- Raw and normalized provider payloads are held transiently and never durably retained or customer-displayed.
- Commercial activation requires a separately reviewed entitlement profile and re-certification.

The versioned limits in code are ELCEO internal safety ceilings, not representations of Tiingo contractual limits.
